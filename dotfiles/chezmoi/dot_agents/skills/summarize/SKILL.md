# Content Summarization Guideline

The key aspect to summarization is to preserve the original meaning
while cutting the flowery language, methaphors and anecdotes around it.

When summarizing content, always gather the full content.

Main ideas are usually extracted when the author restates them in
different forms or using different anecdotes, analogies,
metaphors, or other literary devices.

They are also usually positioned at the beginning
or the end of a paragraph or block of content.

When summarizing content, enumerate the key ideas and main points
in the same sequence as they appear in the original content.

Get rid of any extraneous details or tangential information.

It may be acceptable to include it if it is relevant
to understanding the main points.

Focus on the "generative" idea of the content more than
the "post-factum analysis" ideas that may be present.

Keep language simple and clear, follow STE-100 simplified english,
or other established writing styles.
